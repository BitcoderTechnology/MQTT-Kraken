MQTT Kraken privacy policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

MQTT Kraken is a local Android app for MQTT dashboards and home-screen widgets (package de.bitcoder.mqttkraken).

What the app stores on your device

All configuration stays on the phone. This can include:

  • MQTT broker host, port, client id, and optional username and password
  • Dashboards, tiles, topics, payloads, and optional JavaScript hooks you enter or import
  • Widget layout bindings and last-known MQTT payloads used to refresh widgets
  • Appearance settings such as theme and battery options

Broker passwords are stored in the app database on the device. They are not encrypted at rest in the current version. Android cloud backup of this data is disabled so credentials are not uploaded to Google backup.

What is sent over the network

The app connects only to the MQTT broker you configure. Messages you subscribe to or publish (including from JavaScript hooks) go to that broker. MQTT Kraken does not operate its own cloud backend, accounts, analytics, crash reporting, or advertising.

If you disable TLS, MQTT traffic on your network is not encrypted. Use TLS when the broker supports it, especially off your LAN.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state — connect to your MQTT broker
  • Foreground service (data sync) — keep the MQTT session alive so widgets stay up to date
  • Notifications — required by Android for that foreground service
  • Boot completed — optionally restart the MQTT connection after reboot

Sharing

MQTT Kraken does not sell or share your data with the developer or with advertising partners. The only third party that receives MQTT traffic is the broker you choose.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Retention and deletion

Data remains on the device until you change it in the app or clear the app storage / uninstall MQTT Kraken.

Contact

Privacy questions: use the contact email on the MQTT Kraken Google Play listing.
